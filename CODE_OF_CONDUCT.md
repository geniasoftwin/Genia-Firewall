# Code of Conduct

GeniaFirewall welcomes testing, bug reports, documentation, reviews, and code contributions from people with different levels of experience.

Be respectful and technical. Discuss the code, behavior, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, publication of private data, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Security reports require extra care: do not expose vulnerability details publicly before maintainers have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and fix them. Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these rules and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
